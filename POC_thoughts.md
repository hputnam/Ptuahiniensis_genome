### Ptua--genome from Moorea; haplotype 10

- Most common poc at 20m depth but less common at all other depths (<20m) (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Similar niche breadth to sister taxa Pverr and had same syms (Cladocopium pacificum) (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Multivariate phys in host and sym were not different between Ptua and Pmea (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Associated primarily with C1 and to a lesser extent C42 (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Host responses varied sig between Ptua and Pmea (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Sym biomass and S:H biomass lower in Ptua than Pmea (Huffmyer et al. 2026)

### Pmea--genome from Hawaii; haplotypes 1, 8 (?)

- 377 Mbp, N50 of 10 Mbp, BUSCO score of 94.9% completeness, and 25 642 protein coding genes (Stephens et al. 2022)
- relatively common at 5m depth, well represented at 10m depth, less common at 20m depth in back and fringing reefs (Burgess et al. 2024)
- TPCs from Edmunds et al. 2024 
- Highest high niche breadths and overlaps, tended to host same specialist syms (Cladocopium latusorum) as sister taxa Pgran (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Multivariate phys in host and sym were not different between Ptua and Pmea (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Associated primarily with C42 and to a lesser extent C1 (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Host responses varied sig between Ptua and Pmea (Huffmyer et al. 2026)
- Sym biomass and S:H biomass higher in Pmea than Ptua (Huffmyer et al. 2026)

### Pverr--genome from red sea; haplotype 3

- Relatively uncommon on all depths (<20m) on fore reef, more abundant in 2m backreef (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Growth more rapid than Pmea and Ptua (Burgess & Edmunds 2025)--but hard to determine if this was a carry over effect as they were collected from different locations 
- Similar niche breadth to sister taxa Ptua and had same syms (Cladocopium pacificum) (Burgess et al. 2024)

### Pgran

- Highest high niche breadths and overlaps, tended to host same specialist syms (Cladocopium latusorum) as sister taxa Pmea (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Relatively common 1m fringing, 2m back reef and 5/10m fore reef (Burgess et al. 2024)

### Pacuta--genome from Hawaii

- Narrowest niche breadth and hosted generalist/more thermally tolerant Durusdinium glynnii (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Mostly prevalent in 1m fringing reef 
- Only brooding spp

### Peff--genome from Moorea, haplotype 11

- genome from Noel et al. 2023
- Relatively common at 5, 10, 20m fore reef (Burgess et al. 2024)
- Hosts Cladocopium latusorum (Burgess et al. 2024)
